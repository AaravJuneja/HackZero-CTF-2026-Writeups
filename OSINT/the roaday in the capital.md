# The Roadway in the Capital

## Writeup

The first few things I noticed in this challenge were the Shell gas station and the 7 Eleven convenience store on opposite sides of where we were standing along with a DEAF PEDESTRIAN sign?

The prices of shell gas station was in US dollars which when seen with the fact that the United States have a lot of 7 Elevens I was able to hypothesize that this was somewhere in the United States

The challenge title itself was The Roadway in the Capital which made me think that this was somewhere in Washington D.C. so I then used Google Maps to find a Shell gas station and a 7 Eleven across the street from each other

I found one in the Brookland neighborhood at 3701 12th St NE. I then used the orientation of the images to confirm that this was the correct location but since I am lazy I just typed Shell gas station, 3701 12th St NE :P
